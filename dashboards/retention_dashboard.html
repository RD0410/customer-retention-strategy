Decision-ready findings

Evidence-led next steps, not causal claims
Recommendations
01 · PRIORITIZE

Test retention outreach for flexible-contract fiber customers. This segment combines a substantial sample population with a high historical churn rate.

02 · INVESTIGATE

Explore onboarding experience for new customers. Shorter-tenure cohorts show higher historical churn; survey and support data are needed to identify causes.

03 · MEASURE

Run a randomized retention pilot. Track incremental retention, incentive cost, and long-term value before making a rollout recommendation.
